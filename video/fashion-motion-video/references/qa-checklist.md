# QA checklist (fashion-motion-video)

Check before delivery. A failed item is fixed or dropped, not
explained away.

## Image / start frame

- [ ] Outfit matches the detail page (garment, colour, silhouette,
      distinguishing details such as pocket or flare).
- [ ] Body: 7.5 heads, legs more than half of total height.
- [ ] Model stands on the floor, no pedestal. Background is one
      pick from the Step 2A random pool (clothing store, department
      store / shopping street, park / garden, cafe, street, office,
      home, or plain studio), recorded in the job notes, with clear
      outfit contrast and no distracting sign, logo, or line
      crossing the body.
- [ ] Matching shoes are visible.
- [ ] All hair is behind the shoulders.
- [ ] No info panel covers the model or the outfit.
- [ ] Handwriting, if present in the frame, shows core only
      (product name, price, features) and sits in the side margins.

## Doll mask

- [ ] Mask is the user-supplied doll mask, not a substitute face.
- [ ] Size is 88% of the original large mask size (2026-10-31
      revision 2; the 100% and 70% sizes are superseded).
- [ ] Mask is centred on the eyes, nose, and mouth.
- [ ] Hair behind the shoulders is still visible at the sides.

## New-product texture

- [ ] Outfit reads as brand-new, never washed/worn: no fading,
      matting, flattening, or pilling.
- [ ] Texture fits the material (knit: fluffy, voluminous,
      soft shaggy hair, vivid colour).

## Video

- [ ] Motion lets the viewer judge fit, drape, and details
      (360 rotation, natural movement, model motion, or camera orbit).
- [ ] Product zoom in and zoom out are present.
- [ ] Handwriting shows core only, cleanly, and either stays to
      the end or disappears briefly and reappears, as agreed.
- [ ] No narration voice. Background music only.
- [ ] No info panel covers the model or the outfit at any point.

## Playback limitation

Video playback in this environment cannot prove motion, mask
persistence through movement, or audio. Do not claim those
results from still frames. State what is unverified and ask the
user to check playback for: mask staying on the face, handwriting
staying readable to the end, music present with no narration,
and zoom landing on product details.
