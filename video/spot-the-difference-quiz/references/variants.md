# Variants

> Updated 2026-11-01 (v1.0.0): this file now matches SKILL.md v1.0.0.
> The YouTube Shorts format in SKILL.md is the current standard — the
> 30-second (Medium) / 28-second (Hard) in-video answer reveal, the
> title-end countdown badge, and the soft ding. Anything below that
> contradicts SKILL.md is history only.

## Senior audience

Prefer larger elements, higher contrast, and simpler linework. Use pastel
colors plus one high-chroma accent. Keep on-screen text short, one message
per screen, and keep text areas separate from picture areas. Never promise
a health benefit. Use soft brain-training phrasing only.

Standing senior rules for this series (the senior visibility rule):

- No difference may be a tiny, blurry, far-landscape detail. Make the
  target large enough, colour-distinct, or placed forward enough to be
  clearly visible once circled, including to senior viewers. A distant, small bird in the sky is the
  counter-example: too small and too far to see clearly.
- Countdown digits are large and high-contrast: Warm Grey badge, radius
  48, white Fredoka 54, at the end of the title, never over a picture.
- For a senior-leaning run, prefer Medium early — success comments drive
  the loop. Easy is excluded from this series; the old Easy
  preference is superseded.

This matches the standing senior-legibility principles used elsewhere in
this workspace: large persistent text, calm cuts, and TV-screen framing.

## Younger audience

Keep the brighter and more vivid art direction and vary the backgrounds:
sea, mountains, home, street. Keep the same difficulty rules.

## YouTube Shorts adaptation (current standard, not a variant)

The current Shorts shape is in SKILL.md: 9:16, problem 15 seconds
(5 seconds still + title-end countdown badge 10 to 1), answer
announcement 3 seconds, answers one at a time with a soft ding, BGM bed
throughout, answer revealed inside the video. There is no separate answer
Short and no 12-hour gap.

Safe area: Shorts UI covers the bottom band and a right-side action rail.
Keep text, marks, and key elements out of the bottom 340 pixels and out
of the right ~120 pixels.

## Superseded (do not use)

- v0.2.0 Instagram format: 13-second reel (3-second hold + 10-second
  countdown + loop), no answer in the reel, separate answer carousel
  12 hours later.
- Divider countdown (Warm Grey circle, radius 85, font 72, on the divider
  between pictures) and center-screen countdown — both superseded by the
  title-end badge (radius 48, Fredoka 54).
- Title-end magnifier (v1.8) — removed in v1.9.
- Any answer ding (original, soft, soft2, soft3) — superseded in
  NO answer sound effect. Answer circles appear over the BGM
  bed only.
- The old "second Short as answer" pattern — superseded by the in-video
  answer reveal.
