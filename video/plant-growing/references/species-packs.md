# 종별 프롬프트 팩 — 바로 붙여넣기 (퀵 10초형)

`quickstart-prompts.md`의 템플릿을 종마다 채워 둔 완성 팩. 그대로 복사해 쓴다.
오이 팩은 `quickstart-prompts.md`의 완성 예시 참조.

## 토마토 (tomato)

**첫 프레임**
```
Ultra-realistic macro photograph of a single tomato seed just placed beneath the soil surface inside a transparent glass pot, rich dark potting soil, soft even studio lighting, clean black background, fixed camera angle from slightly above, shallow depth of field, highly detailed texture of seed and soil, photorealistic, 4K, no hands, no text, no watermark
```

**중간/성장 프레임**
```
Ultra-realistic macro photograph of a young tomato plant growing inside a transparent glass pot, serrated green leaves unfurling on a slender stem, fine white roots visible through the glass, same exact pot, same soil level, same black background, same fixed camera angle and lighting as previous frame, highly detailed, photorealistic, 4K
```

**마지막 프레임**
```
Ultra-realistic macro photograph of a mature tomato plant with several healthy serrated green leaves, small yellow flowers and one cluster of ripe red cherry tomatoes inside a transparent glass pot, white roots clearly visible, same exact transparent glass pot, same soil, same clean black background, same fixed camera angle and soft even lighting, photorealistic, 4K, no text
```

**비디오 프롬프트**
```
Smooth cinematic 10-second time-lapse of a tomato seed growing inside a transparent glass pot. Start with the seed just under the soil surface. The seed slowly swells and cracks open, a tiny white root grows downward, a green shoot emerges and rises, serrated leaves unfurl and expand naturally, small yellow flowers bloom and tiny red cherry tomatoes begin to form and ripen. Camera remains completely fixed, soil and pot stay unchanged, soft even lighting, no flicker, highly realistic plant growth, photorealistic, macro detail, natural motion, 9:16 vertical aspect ratio
```

**마지막 프레임**

## 새 종 팩 추가 규칙

1. `quickstart-prompts.md` 템플릿의 문형을 그대로 쓰고, 종 이름 + 종 특성어(잎 모양·색·최종 형태)만 교체한다
2. 최종 형태가 "열매"가 아닌 종(나무 묘목·잎채소)은 마지막 프레임·비디오 프롬프트에서 열매 문구를 빼고 그 종의 최종 볼거리로 바꾼다
3. 키가 큰 종은 첫 프레임에 프레이밍 여유 문구(`generous empty space above`, `stays inside the frame`)를 넣는다
4. 팩을 추가하면 `species-cards.md`에도 해당 종의 키워드·상품 매핑을 함께 적는다
