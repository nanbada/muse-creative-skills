# Type rules — place-type adaptation

Each type lists the questions that surface "what is unique here" and the
generic scenes that must never pass the swap test. Add new types as they are
encountered; keep the question format (2–3 per type).

| Type | Uniqueness questions | Banned generic scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 서점/매장 (bookstore/store) | 1. What is unique about the space layout, displays, circulation? 2. Signature products or corners? 3. Relationship between the building and the view outside the windows? | Page-turning, paying at the register, panning random shelves |
| 대학 (university) | 1. What buildings / campus terrain identify this school? 2. Signature facilities, labs, libraries, traditions? 3. Seasonal scenery unique to this campus? | Laptop screens, scribbling pens, note-taking in a generic classroom |
| 식당/카페 (restaurant/cafe) | 1. Signature menu and its cooking process? 2. Actual structure of kitchen, seating, windows? 3. Tableware, ingredients, sounds unique here? | Generic food close-up rotation, toasting, random menus |
| 경기장 (stadium) | 1. Structure, seats, lighting, roof? 2. Cheering culture and rituals? 3. Home-team colors and symbols? | Generic crowd cheering, ball close-ups, scoreboard numbers |
| 랜드마크/관광명소 (landmark) | 1. Instantly recognizable silhouette / angles? 2. Experiences only possible here? 3. Signature light by time of day / season? | Selfies, tourists looking at maps, sky and clouds |
| 박물관/미술관 (museum) | 1. Architecture, circulation, signature exhibition spaces? 2. Signature holdings? (check public / photo-allowed status) 3. Distinctive way of viewing? | Back view standing before a painting, generic curator talk |
| 공원/자연 (park/nature) | 1. Unique terrain, tree species, water composition? 2. Signature scenes by season / time? 3. Activities or rules only found here? | Walking a generic forest path, sitting on a bench, birdsong |
| 호텔/숙소 (hotel) | 1. Recognizable rooms, views, architecture, lobby? 2. Signature facilities and services? 3. Flow of time from check-in through the stay? | Generic bed-making, room-service carts, pulling a suitcase |
| 전통시장/거리 (market/street) | 1. Alley structure, signs, roof shapes unique here? 2. Signature foods / goods and how they are cooked / sold? 3. Sounds unique to vendors and shoppers here? | Generic haggling, ordinary vegetable displays |
| 공연장/문화시설 (venue) | 1. Stage, seating, lobby structure unique here? 2. Signature performances or programs? 3. Rituals from entry to showtime? | Audience only clapping, generic light shows |

## Overseas-place rules (apply on top of the type)

- Write the local-language original alongside the Korean rendering.
- Reflect local signage script, traffic direction, dress norms, and etiquette
  from verified sources only — never stereotypes.
- Flag possible shooting / commercial-use restrictions in block B.
- For sacred or culturally sensitive sites, prefer wide respectful framing and
  note conduct rules the prompter must respect.
