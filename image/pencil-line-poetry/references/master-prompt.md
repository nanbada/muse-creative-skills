Paste-ready English prompt template (Grok Imagine, Midjourney, Flux,

## Mode A — Tiny line world (default, dominant pattern)

A tiny line-drawn girl with minimal dot eyes, a small gentle smile,
and simple dress {{character_detail, e.g. "with braided hair, a small
dog beside her"}} {{action, e.g. "launching a paper boat"}} inside an
oversized photorealistic nature macro world: {{giant_nature, e.g.
"a giant dew-covered ginkgo leaf arching over rippling water"}}.
Fine delicate pencil lines for the character; photorealistic macro
detail for nature — dewdrops like crystal balls, translucent petals,
moss and water texture. {{texture_option: "subtle thread-embroidery
hair and paper-cut petal dress" / omit}}. Warm golden-hour
backlight, sun flare, glowing edges. Vertical composition, generous
negative space, one quiet poetic moment. Healing, whimsical,
contemplative mood.

## Mode B — Glow lines

Luminous line art: {{subject, e.g. "a girl with a hat"}} drawn in
glowing {{line_color: "warm golden-white" / "red"}} lines
{{setting, e.g. "standing in a sunlit lavender field with luminous

## Mode C — Line over landscape

A translucent, delicately line-drawn woman {{pose, e.g. "standing on
a giant feather floating on a dusk lake"}} in a photorealistic
landscape: {{landscape, e.g. "misty mountains, still water, pastel
sunset sky"}}. Fine pencil lines; her dress and hair dissolving into
light. Magical realism, serene and poetic. Vertical, generous
negative space.

## Mode D — Soft painterly

Soft semi-realistic painting of {{subject, e.g. "a woman resting on
a forest bench"}}, hazy {{light, e.g. "morning"}} light, pastel
palette, gentle anime-influenced illustration. Calm, warm,
contemplative. The most realistic variant — use only when asked.

## Video prompt (append to any mode)

Subtle animation only: {{motion, e.g. "petals drifting down,
dewdrops glistening, hair stirring in a gentle breeze, butterflies
hovering, light flickering"}}. Slow peaceful drift, 6–15 seconds,
seamless quiet loop. No scene changes, no fast movement.

## Filling guide

- Keep the character SMALL and nature GIANT (mode A) — that scale
play is the signature.
- Character face: dot eyes or closed eyes, tiny smile. Never
detailed anime features.
- Reinforcement keywords when the generator drifts: "fine delicate
line art, minimal facial features, dot eyes, tiny character in a
giant nature macro world, translucent petals, watercolor wash,
golden-hour backlight, generous negative space, healing poetic
mood".
- Series tip: fix the seed, keep the style block identical, vary
only the nature element, season, or light.
