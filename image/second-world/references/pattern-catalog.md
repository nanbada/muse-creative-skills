# Second World — Pattern Catalog

Distilled from 22 user-supplied reference posters (2026-10-07).
Use this when choosing the continuity structure and interaction for a new
photo: pick the family that fits, then invent an image-specific variation —
never copy an existing poster's interaction mechanically.

## Structure families → interaction types

### 1. Path / road / track → continue the journey
- `04-a-little-more-road`: desert road → pencil-drawn line ending at a real pencil left on the paper
- `05-railway-scenic-route`: train tracks → hand-drawn rails being **built/laid** by 2 figures (construction)
- `18-trail-no-caption`: mountain trail → drawn trail with a hiker (no caption — allowed when the image speaks)
- Moves: extend as drawing, show construction in progress, leave the tool behind

### 2. Water → handle it
- `02-whats-under-here`: canal water → paper **peeled back** to reveal the water
- `11-rockarch-downstream`: arch water → narrow stream being **unrolled** like paper
- `20-coffee-for-a-walk`: spilled coffee → winding river **walked like a dog** on a leash
- Moves: peel, unroll, leash — water is a material you can touch

### 3. Reflection → underwater world ("Same X, deeper Y")
- `07-ship-deeper-stories`: night ship reflection → underwater scene (whale, diver, fish)
- `13-clouds-deeper-waters`: clouds → underwater cloud-forms (whale, diver, fish)
- `09-lake-worth-diving`: lake surface → torn-paper underwater cross-section (diver, fish, rocks)
- Moves: the lower half reveals what is beneath; torn edge shaped like a dive cut

### 4. Hanging thing → swing
- `01-spring-break`: branch → petal swing
- `16-cosmos-small-joys`: flower stem → petal swing
- `10-nyc-ill-wait-here`: bridge suspension cables → swing ropes
- Moves: anything that hangs can hold a swing; figure usually passive/contemplative

### 5. Light → make it material
- `12-forest-borrowing-light`: dappled light path → torn paper path figures walk on
- `15-streetlight-falling-leaves`: lamp beam → visible cone catching falling leaves
- `22-moon-comes-closer`: moonlight reflection → path leading down to a cat
- Moves: light becomes walkable, visible, or a guide

### 6. One small verb (pull / tug / hang)
- `03-just-a-little-tug`: ship rope → tugged rope, straining figure
- `06-clock-afternoon`: clock chains → **pulled** to stretch the afternoon (2 figures)
- `14-bird-morning-song`: bird's breath vapor → ribbon **hung out like laundry**
- Moves: single verb, single figure, deadpan understatement

### 7. Minimal — structure alone
- `17-umbrella-rain-check`: flower stem → one droplet. No figures.
- `21-mist-lets-you-in`: mist → ink-wash lines, one tiny figure
- Moves: restraint; when the structure is eloquent, add nothing

### 8. Street → crossing
- `19-city-another-way`: street → watercolor flooded street with stepping stones, figure crossing
- Moves: the familiar route becomes a small obstacle

### 9. Shore → gentle extension
- `08-beach-further-please`: waves → extended watercolor shoreline, girl + dog watching
- Moves: "a little further" — contemplative, not playful

## Caption style guide

- Mostly lowercase, handwritten, one short line. Based on the action.
- **The "a little" understatement device** (recurring signature): "just a
  little tug", "a little more road", "A little further please", "We pulled
  a little more afternoon", "Just a little downstream"
- **The "Same X, deeper Y" device** (reflection family): "Same harbour,
  deeper stories", "same clouds, deeper waters"
- Short declaratives: "spring break.", "Rain check.", "I'll wait here.",
  "Moon comes closer.", "Borrowing Light", "Worth diving for", "Another
  way to the city", "Taking the scenic route.", "Taking my iced coffee
  for a walk.", "the mist lets you in", "Hanging the morning song out to
  air.", "Streetlight for falling leaves.", "small joys go a long way"
- Caption is optional (`18-trail` has none).

## Figure notes

- 0–2 figures typical across all 22 (max: railway, clock, beach).
- Figures always **physically interact** with the continued structure:
  pulling, building, hanging, walking, crossing, sitting/watching.
  Never decorative.
- Recurring contemplative girl (swing series, streetlight, beach).
- Animals appear as subjects, not decoration: dog (beach), cat (moon),
  whale/diver/fish (underwater series).
- If the source photo already has strong human action, add none.
